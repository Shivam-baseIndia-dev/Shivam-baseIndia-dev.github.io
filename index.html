About us

Base India is headquartered in Gurgaon, the developing financial capital of India. We've carved out a niche in structural steel detailing and allied services, with over 500 projects to our name. We started small — a team of two engineers in 2004 — and grew on the trust of clients who came back project after project.

Within a short span we were handling structures from 300 to 2000 tons, on completion cycles of 3 to 12 weeks. Today we're a team capable of running 7–10 projects of varying size and complexity at once, led by managers who push the work further with every job.

Our strength

Our strength is accurate, error-free detailing that puts clients at ease on the field. A state-of-the-art workstation, current industry software, and a methodical process — assessment, planning, tailor-made drawings, general detailing — mean fabricators get work done faster, within budget, and without the stress.

We work to AISC and CISC standards for commercial and industrial structures, with added expertise in IS, BS, and Australian codes. Our client base spans India, the US and Canada, and we're built to take on new geographies as they come.

Sheet 02

Services

From anchor bolts to advance bills of material — the full detailing scope.

S-01 / STRUCTURAL

Steel Drafting & Detailing

  • Anchor bolt setting plans & shop drawings for anchor bolts and levelling plates
  • Embed layouts and details, with shop drawings for embed plates and angles
  • Erection/framing plans at all floors and roof, with shop drawings for columns, beams and bracings
  • Elevations with all bracing and connection details
  • Sections with field-work information — field bolting and welding
  • Reports covering material lists, NC & DXF files, KSS files and shop/field bolt lists
S-02 / MISC.

Architectural Miscellaneous

  • Stair erection plans & elevations, with shop drawings for stairs, rails, landing frames and supports
  • Ladder layouts — elevator pit, roof access and cage ladders
  • Handrail and guardrail layouts with shop drawings
  • Grating and floor plate layouts with shop drawings
  • Joist modelling, layout and joist listing
  • Deck layouts and deck listing
S-03 / CONNECTIONS

Steel Connection Design

  • Connection design, review and analysis handled by a dedicated team
  • Modelled and checked with StaadPro and current industry software
  • Supports commercial and industrial structures to AISC / CISC / IS / BS / AS codes
S-04 / ESTIMATION

Estimations

  • Competitive bids on every project type — no guesswork on cost or performance
  • Each job worked on specific man-hour cost and item method for accuracy
  • Advance Bill of Material (ABM) and material take-offs generated straight from 3D models
